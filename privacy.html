Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

"AI Khabri Uploader" is a private application used only by the owner of the AI Khabri YouTube channel to upload, schedule and caption videos on that channel and to read that channel's own statistics.

What data the app uses

  • Through the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission: uploading videos, thumbnails and captions, updating video details, and reading the channel's own video statistics and analytics.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users.
  • We do not access any YouTube account other than the AI Khabri channel.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings.
